Services I offer

SAP, Dynamics, a new process or Copilot. Every new system pays off only when people use it in their daily work.

The licence is paid on day one. The value comes when people can do their real work with it. I build the learning and practice layer that gets them there, end to end: change, training, e-learning and practice on the job, as one package.

How I work

Four stages, from the first conversation to the day your team runs the work without me. You can start small, with one course or one team.

  1. 01

    Agree the scope in writing

    I start with your problem and the systems you already have. I talk to subject matter experts, managers and some of the learners, and I look at the current platform and its data.

    You get a short scope to sign, and every later change is checked against it. On the Advanced Learning World build, one Excel scope sheet was the master document for the vendor.

  2. 02

    Design around what you already run

    Learning design and technical design run together. I map the learner journey, the content and the integrations (LMS, SCORM, xAPI, sign-in) at the same time, so no design depends on a feature your platform cannot deliver.

    I adapt proven platforms before anyone writes new ones, and I prototype early in the real tool so reviewers can click through it. In 2019 I advised Advanced Learning Company against a long build from scratch. Their Open edX platform went live in September 2020, 3.5 months after delivery started.

  3. 03

    Pilot with real users before launch

    I build in Storyline, Rise, Open edX, Moodle or Drupal, depending on what you already run. AI tools such as Synthesia and text-to-speech come in where they save time and budget. Translation covers the navigation, messages and prompts as well as the script.

    A pilot finds what reviews miss. The three-country SITCIN pilot showed silent data loss and overwritten records, so I set one National Focal Point per country and moved the pilot to Excel import with verification. All three assessments were submitted and closed.

  4. 04

    Hand it over documented

    Launch covers the communications, testing, publishing on your LMS and the first round of fixes.

    The work is finished when your team can run it without me. On SITCIN the original developer had left no admin documentation, so I wrote the QZ Manager guide, handed the git repository to UNECE IT and worked through the deployment blockers with them.
