Vilniaus miesto savivaldybė

Vilnius City Municipality. Code 13. This is the city municipality, not Vilniaus rajono savivaldybė (code 41).

County: Vilnius County (Vilniaus apskritis), NUTS 3 LT011.

County residents on 1 January 2024: 868 251. That is 30.1% of Lithuania’s 2 885 891 residents on the same day, calculated from those two Eurostat figures. The county figure covers every municipality in the county, not this city alone.

County source: Eurostat demo_r_pjangrp3 for LT011. Dataset updated 28 August 2026.
