Municipalities

Lithuania has 60 municipalities. A city municipality and a district municipality can share a place name. They are different legal areas, with different codes.

Vilniaus miesto savivaldybė (code 13) is not Vilniaus rajono savivaldybė (code 41). The same split is in the list for Kaunas (19 and 52) and Klaipėda (21 and 55).

School Atlas tags a campus with a city name such as Vilnius. In this file that tag is stored only on the city municipality. The district municipality with the same name has no School Atlas city tag.
