Five ways to show who, what and when

The same projects from metabrain in three new forms, followed by the two charts used on the homepage so far. Everything before NSW Community Services (February 2012) is combined into one item, C-Learning / projects. Detail starts with NSW. Pick one (or a mix) and I will put it on the homepage in place of the radar timeline. This page is temporary and hidden from search engines.

1

Project timeline by organisation

One row per organisation, one bar per project, from NSW Community Services in 2012 to today, with one grey bar for C-Learning and the projects before it. Reads like a CV at a glance: who you worked for, for how long, and how the work overlapped. UN and international work in red.

Hover a bar for the project and dates. Scroll on the chart or drag the bar above it to zoom into a period.

2

Months of work per year, by sector

Stacked columns: each year's total months of project work, split by sector. The first column, C-Learning / projects, shows the yearly average before 2012. Shows the shape of the career: agency and corporate work in Australia and the UK, then the growth of UN and international work after 2016.

Hover a column for the breakdown. Click a sector in the legend to hide or show it.

3

Where the time went

A treemap: each block is an organisation, its size the total months of work, grouped by sector. The grey block, C-Learning / projects, holds everything before 2012. Click a block to open its projects. Shows at once that OSCE and UNECE are the long-term relationships.

Click an organisation to see its projects; use the path at the top to go back.

4

Radar timeline (on the homepage now)

The chart currently on the preview homepage. Each column is a project, grouped by organisation on the outer ring, its height the months of work in the chosen year.

Drag the slider or press play to move through the years. Click an organisation on the outer ring to zoom in.

5

30 organisations (earlier homepage)

The chart the homepage had before the radar timeline: one dot for each organisation, coloured by sector.

Hover or tap a dot to see the organisation.

Measure: months of work per project per year, from the dates in metabrain (work history and the OSCE project file). Bayer and Metability are left out. Charts by amCharts 5 (free licence, logo shown).
