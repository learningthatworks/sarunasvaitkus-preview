Getting people ready for a new system on day one: CRM and Coupa rollouts at KPMG and CBRE

The situation

A system migration fails when people can't do their job in the new system on the first morning. At KPMG the Business Change team was moving the UK firm to Microsoft Dynamics CRM and Coupa, while Finance, Risk and Audit needed their own learning. At CBRE the business had just implemented Coupa, and the Change and Communications team still trained mostly face to face.

How I approached it

I treated the learning as part of the change plan. That meant reading the change impact assessments before writing anything, working out which tasks each group would actually do in the new system, and lining every module up with the communication campaign.

Decisions

  1. KPMG

    Start from the change impact assessment

    I reviewed the change impact assessments with the change team and ran skills-gap analysis, so the learning covered what changed for each group.

  2. KPMG

    Run UAT and UX workshops

    I ran UAT and UX workshops with the change team as part of the rollout, alongside the learning design.

  3. KPMG

    Train in step with the communications

    Training objectives followed the planned communication campaigns, and I helped the communications team with content and graphics, so the messages and the modules matched.

  4. CBRE

    Show me, try me, test me

    For myBuy, the purchase order training, each task appears three ways: a recorded walkthrough, a practice simulation, and a short test.

  5. CBRE

    Move face-to-face training online

    I helped the Change and Communications team plan the move from classroom sessions to online learning, and built an onboarding programme for their permanent and temporary staff.

What shipped

  • Self-paced modules, assessments and job aids in Storyline, Captivate, iSpring and Rise
  • System simulations recorded from the live software
  • Explainer and communication videos, including VideoScribe and Synthesia
  • Coupa modules at CBRE in English and French
  • A Change and Communications onboarding programme at CBRE
  • Deployment through SuccessFactors and SharePoint at KPMG, and through the LMS, Teams and SharePoint at CBRE

My part

  • Training needs and skills-gap analysis, and review of change impact assessments
  • Curriculum, storyboards, scripts and instructional writing
  • Visual design of templates, navigation and interactions
  • Production of modules, simulations and videos
  • Testing, deploying and publishing SCORM on the client LMS, with admin rights the client granted for that work, and tracking completions

Results

These programmes were internal, and I don't hold usage or completion figures for them, so I don't quote any. What the record shows: KPMG extended my contract in 2018 and again in 2019, and I stayed until the handover in October 2019. The demos linked on this page are modules from these programmes.

What I would do again, or differently

  • System training lands when it is timed with the communications campaign.
  • Give people a way to practise the task in a simulation before they are tested on it.
