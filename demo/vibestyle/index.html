Why this exists

Why AI websites all look the same

Open three AI-built landing pages and count the purple gradients. The same indigo button shows up, with Inter underneath and four tidy cards in a row. A hover state you can barely see finishes the job. Models hand back the average of everything they learned from, and the average of the internet is a polite shade of grey.

You can push back today, even if you have never heard the words Swiss Design or Claymorphism. Pick a style you'd never choose and see what happens. Copy its prompt into Claude, Cursor, Lovable or whatever you build with. When the page comes back, change one thing by hand and show it to a friend before you polish it.
