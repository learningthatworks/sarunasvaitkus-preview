Edit­orial

How the craft of magazine layout, with its columns, pull quotes and dramatic type, gives web pages a voice and a reading rhythm.

A field guide · 12 minute read

Editorial design treats a page as a story. The designer decides what the reader sees first, where the eye rests and how long a line runs before it breaks. Type size shifts dramatically between headline and body, and white space frames the most important words.

Layout is a form of pacing. It tells readers when to slow down.

Read the kit Copy the prompt

What defines it

Editorial design brings the craft of magazines and newspapers to screens. It uses dramatic type scale, columns and pacing to guide reading.

Dramatic type scale

Headlines can be ten times the size of body text. The contrast creates hierarchy without decoration.

Columns and rules

Text flows in columns separated by thin rules, and heavy rules open new sections.

Reading devices

Drop caps, decks, bylines and pull quotes help readers enter, skim and return to long text.

Art direction

Images are cropped and placed with intent, often bleeding off edges or breaking the grid.

Where it came from

Editorial design grew up in print. The web inherited its tools once CSS could handle columns and serious typography.

  1. 1934

    Brodovitch

    Alexey Brodovitch becomes art director of Harper's Bazaar and turns magazine spreads into dynamic compositions of type, photography and white space.

  2. 1959

    Twen

    Willy Fleckhaus designs the German magazine Twen with oversized images and a strict grid, influencing generations of art directors.

  3. 1992

    Ray Gun

    David Carson's layouts for Ray Gun break every rule of legibility and show that editorial design can be expressive.

  4. 2012

    Snow Fall

    The New York Times publishes Snow Fall, a long-form story with integrated media that sets a new standard for editorial web design.

  5. 2017

    CSS Grid

    Major browsers ship CSS Grid, making magazine-style layouts possible without hacks.

Motion study

Editorial motion serves reading. Words reveal at reading pace and rules draw in to mark emphasis.

Design is the pace at which we read.

Reveal words at about 300ms each, close to natural reading speed.

Signature layout: Text columns

Long text flows across columns with a rule between them.

Editorial columns let long text breathe on wide screens. The column-width property sets a minimum, so on phones everything collapses to a single readable column without media queries. Rules between columns add structure without weight.

Balance a long article with one pull quote per screen and generous margins.

Common mistakes

Where this style usually goes wrong, and how to keep it honest.

Columns you have to scroll

Multi-column text on screen fails if readers must scroll up to read the next column. Keep column blocks short.

Serif soup

Three serifs fighting each other. Pick one display and one text face.

Tiny body text

Magazine print sizes are too small for screens. Body text should be 17 to 20 pixels.

Decoration without story

Drop caps and pull quotes only work on real long-form content.
