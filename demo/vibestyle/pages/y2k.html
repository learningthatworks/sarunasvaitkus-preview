Y2K

The look of the year 2000. Liquid chrome, glossy bubble buttons and translucent plastic promised a shiny digital future, and Gen Z brought it back two decades later.

Enter the future Copy prompt

What defines it

Y2K design celebrates technology with gloss and shine. Everything looks like polished plastic or liquid metal, lit by an optimistic future.

Liquid chrome

Metallic gradients with a hard horizon line through the middle turn type and icons into reflective metal.

Glossy bubbles

Buttons and orbs have a white highlight on top and a darker bottom, like candy or water drops.

Translucent plastic

Frosted, tinted surfaces recall the see-through gadgets of the late 1990s.

Tech-futurist type

Wide, extended sans-serifs with a space-age feel, often set in capitals.

Where it came from

Y2K is named after the millennium bug panic, but its look came from optimism about computers and the new century.

  1. 1998

    See-through computers

    Apple's translucent iMac G3 makes colored plastic the look of consumer tech.

  2. 1999

    Chrome futures

    Music videos, movie titles and sportswear lean on chrome, silver and blue light as symbols of the new millennium.

  3. 2000

    The bug that never bit

    The year 2000 arrives without the feared computer crash, and the era gets its nickname.

  4. 2001+

    Glossy interfaces

    Operating systems and media players adopt shiny blue buttons, bubbles and aqua gel effects.

  5. 2019+

    Gen Z revival

    Fashion, TikTok and music artwork revive chrome type and butterflies, and the web follows.

Motion study

Y2K motion loves orbits and shine. Things spin, float and sparkle as if powered by a gadget.

Loops should feel like a screensaver: smooth, endless and a little hypnotic.

Common mistakes

Where this style usually goes wrong, and how to keep it honest.

Chrome text in paragraphs

Metallic gradients are for display type only. Body text needs a solid color.

Gloss on everything

If every surface shines, nothing does. Keep the full effect for buttons and hero objects.

Low contrast silver

Silver on ice blue can disappear. Check contrast on every label.

Costume nostalgia

Stacking butterflies, flip phones and sparkles turns into parody. Pick one or two references.
