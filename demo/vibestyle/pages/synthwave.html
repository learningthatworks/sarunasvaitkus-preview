Synthwave

Retro future

A look built on memories of the 1980s. Neon grids run toward a striped sunset, and chrome letters shine as if lit from a dashboard.

Enter the grid Copy prompt

What defines it

Synthwave started as a music genre. The visuals grew from album covers and remix a handful of 1980s motifs again and again.

Perspective grid

A wireframe floor stretching to the horizon, from early computer graphics when a grid was all a machine could render fast.

Striped sunset

A gradient sun sliced by horizontal bands, probably the most recognisable synthwave symbol.

Neon glow

Magenta and cyan light on deep purple night. Layered shadows fake the halo of a gas tube.

Chrome lettering

Metallic italic type with a hard horizon line through the middle, borrowed from 1980s film titles.

Where it came from

Synthwave is nostalgia for a future people imagined forty years ago.

  1. 1982

    Neon cinema

    Tron shows glowing grids inside a computer. The same year, Blade Runner pairs neon cities with Vangelis's synthesizer score.

  2. 1984

    Miami Vice

    The TV series pushes pastel suits and pink neon onto prime time.

  3. 1986

    Out Run

    Sega's arcade driving game sends players toward sunsets in a sports car. Its name becomes outrun, a label for the whole scene.

  4. 2000s

    The revival

    Producers such as Kavinsky and College rebuild 1980s synth sounds, and online communities share the look alongside the music.

  5. 2011

    Drive

    The film Drive uses tracks by Kavinsky and College, and the sound reaches a mainstream audience.

  6. 2010s+

    Everywhere

    Games like Far Cry 3: Blood Dragon (2013), album art and web design adopt the grid and the sunset as shorthand for retro fun.

Motion study

Synthwave motion is an endless drive. Loops never resolve: the road keeps coming and the sun never sets.

A dashed line and two scaling palms are enough to suggest speed.

Common mistakes

Where this style usually goes wrong, and how to keep it honest.

Glow on body text

Blurred halos make paragraphs hard to read. Keep glow for short headings.

Thin fonts on dark

Hairline display fonts vanish at small sizes. Check legibility on a phone at arm's length.

Endless motion

Rolling grids can trigger motion sickness. Honour reduced motion and offer a pause control.

Cliché overload

Sun, grid, palms, car and chrome all at once becomes parody. Pick two motifs.
