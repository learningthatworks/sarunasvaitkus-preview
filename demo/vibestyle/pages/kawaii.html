Kawaii

The Japanese culture of cute. Round shapes, soft pastels and tiny happy faces on everything, from rice balls to buttons, make an interface feel warm and huggable.

Open the kit Copy prompt
♡♡✦✦

What defines it

Kawaii means cute in Japanese. As a design style it uses round shapes, pastel colours, thick soft outlines and small faces to create affection and delight.

Faces on things

Two dot eyes, blushing cheeks and a tiny mouth can turn any object into a character.

Round, chubby shapes

Everything is soft and rounded, with short proportions and no sharp corners.

Pastel palette

Pink, mint, lilac and butter yellow with a warm brown outline.

Thick soft outlines

A consistent rounded outline and a small solid shadow make shapes look like stickers.

Where it came from

Kawaii grew from a youth trend in Japan into one of the world's most recognisable design cultures.

  1. 1970s

    Cute handwriting

    Teenagers in Japan popularise a round, playful handwriting style decorated with hearts and stars.

  2. 1974

    Character goods

    Sanrio introduces Hello Kitty, and cute character merchandise becomes a huge industry.

  3. 1990s

    Street culture

    Harajuku fashion and kawaii stationery spread the look across Asia and beyond.

  4. 1999

    Emoji

    Shigetaka Kurita designs a set of tiny pictograms for Japanese mobile phones, an ancestor of today's emoji.

  5. 2020s

    Global cute

    Apps, games and brands worldwide use kawaii mascots to feel friendly.

Motion study

Kawaii motion is bouncy and alive. Characters squash, bounce and blink, and little hearts float away.

A blink every few seconds makes a character feel alive with almost no effort.

Prompt it

A ready prompt for AI design and coding tools. It names exact tokens and bans the usual defaults, so the output keeps this style's point of view.

Design a responsive landing page for a kids' reading app in an authentic Kawaii style.

Palette: Cream #FFF6F0, Pink #FF8FAB, Blush #FFB5C8, Mint #B8F0D8, Lilac #D9C8FF, Butter #FFE8A3, Cocoa #5A3E36.
Typography: Mochiy Pop One for titles, M PLUS Rounded 1c for body.
Layout: Two-column hero on cream with pink polka dots: a chunky pink outlined title, intro and buttons on the left, an original round rice-ball character with a face that bounces and blinks on the right, with floating hearts and stars.
Signature details: Pastel palette outlined in cocoa brown (#5A3E36), 2.5px outlines, solid 5px drop shadows, large radius, tiny faces on cards.
Components: Pill buttons that bounce, cards with a tiny face on top, butter-yellow heart badges, striped pink progress bars.
Motion: Squash and stretch bounce, a quick blink every four seconds, floating hearts.
Avoid: copies of famous characters, sharp corners, black outlines, pastel text.
