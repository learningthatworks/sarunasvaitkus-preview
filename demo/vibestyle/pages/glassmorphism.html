Glass­morphism

Interfaces made of frosted glass. Panels blur whatever sits behind them, so vivid color glows through while content stays readable on top.

Open the kit Copy prompt
Balance€ 12,480Frosted panel, blur 20px

What defines it

Glassmorphism layers translucent panels over colorful backgrounds. Depth comes from blur and light edges, like looking through frosted glass.

Background blur

Panels use backdrop-filter to blur and slightly saturate whatever sits behind them.

Translucent fill

Surfaces are white at 10 to 25 percent opacity, often with a subtle diagonal gradient.

Light edges

A thin semi-transparent white border catches the light and defines the pane.

Vivid backdrop

The effect needs color behind it. Soft gradient blobs or photos give the glass something to frost.

Where it came from

Frosted glass has come back to interfaces several times, each time when hardware could finally afford the blur.

  1. 2007

    Aero glass

    Windows Vista introduces translucent, blurred window frames, one of the first mass-market uses of glass in UI.

  2. 2013

    iOS 7

    Apple replaces skeuomorphic textures with flat design and frosted, blurred layers.

  3. 2017

    Acrylic

    Microsoft's Fluent Design System formalises a blurred acrylic material for Windows apps.

  4. 2020

    A name for it

    Designer Michał Malewicz popularises the term glassmorphism, and macOS Big Sur leans heavily on translucency the same year.

  5. 2025

    Liquid Glass

    Apple introduces Liquid Glass across its platforms, bringing refractive, moving glass effects into system UI.

Motion study

Glass motion happens behind the pane. Colors drift slowly underneath, and the frosted surface stays still and calm.

Still glass

Keep the glass static and move the background. Moving glass on top quickly feels busy.

Common mistakes

Where this style usually goes wrong, and how to keep it honest.

Unreadable text

Busy backgrounds bleed through. Increase blur or opacity until text passes contrast checks everywhere.

No backdrop

Glass over a flat color looks like gray plastic. The style needs color or imagery behind it.

Performance

Many large blurred layers can drain battery on phones. Limit the number of glass panes in view.

Browser support

Provide a solid semi-opaque fallback for browsers or settings that disable backdrop-filter.
