Clean Girl Brutalism

A lot of white, stark black grotesk, and a single soft pink block on a raw grid.

See the grid Copy the prompt
01notespink

What defines it

Clean girl brutalism keeps the blunt grid of brutalist web design and throws away the loud colour. What remains is white space, black type and one pink rectangle.

Raw grid

Lines stay visible. Sections are cells, not floating cards with gaps.

Stark grotesk

Black, tight, heavy. One family for the headline and the note.

One pink block

A single soft pink rectangle. It is the only colour on the page.

Empty space

Most cells are white. Filling them is how the style fails.

Where it came from

It borrows the Swiss grid and the bluntness of brutalist websites, then quiets the colour down to one blush block.

  1. 1950s

    The grid shows

    Swiss posters let the measurement stay visible.

  2. 2000s

    Fashion sites

    Clothing brands use huge type and almost empty pages.

  3. 2018

    Loud brutalism

    Web brutalism adds thick outlines, hard shadows and many colours.

  4. 2022

    A quieter branch

    Some portfolios keep the raw grid but return to white, black and one blush block.

  5. Now

    The name

    People call that branch clean girl brutalism: spare, stark, one soft accent.

Motion study

The pink block steps one cell to the right and stops. No bounce, no fade.

The step should feel like a layout change, not like a toy.

Prompt it

A ready prompt for Lovable and Claude Design. It names the palette, type, layout, texture, what to do and what to avoid, so the output keeps this style's point of view.

Style prompt for Lovable and Claude Design. Design a responsive landing page for a beauty editor's notes site in an authentic Clean Girl Brutalism style.

Palette: White #FFFFFF, Ink #111111, Soft pink #F4C3CB, Hairline #E4E4E4. Pink appears once.
Typography: Inter, weight 900 for the headline, 400 to 500 for reading. Tight tracking on titles.
Layout: A visible three-by-three grid. The headline sits in a cell. One cell is the pink block. Most cells stay empty.
Texture: None. Hairline grid only. No grain, no shadow, no blur.
Signature details: Zero radius, zero shadow, 1px black rules, one pink fill.
Components: Black rectangular buttons, white cards with a 1px rule, a pink badge.
Motion: The pink block steps across cells with a steps timing function. It does not bounce.
Do: Leave most of the page white. Use black grotesk. Use the pink once, as a block.
Avoid: Thick meme outlines, hard offset shadows, more than one accent colour, gradients, rounded cards, serifs.
Do not fall back on generic AI defaults: no centered hero with a gradient blob, no identical rounded cards with soft grey shadows, no purple-to-blue gradients, no emoji icons, unless this style explicitly calls for them.
Accessibility: body text contrast of at least 4.5:1, visible keyboard focus on every control, labels on all form fields, and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
Deliver one self-contained HTML file with CSS variables for every color, font, radius and shadow token.

Common mistakes

Where this style usually goes wrong, and how to keep it honest.

Neo-brutalism

Thick black offsets and five bright colours are a different style. This one is quiet.

Pink type

The pink is a block. Painting the headline pink loses the stark black.

Filled grid

If every cell has content, the white space is gone and so is the style.

Soft shadows

A grey shadow makes it look like a template. Keep the edges hard and flat.
